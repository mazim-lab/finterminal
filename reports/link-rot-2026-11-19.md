# Link-rot sentinel report, 2026-11-19

Checked 125 front-facing links: 8 deal URLs with no `expiresAt` in `src/data/deals.ts`, 100 `sourceUrl` links in `src/data/news.ts`, and 17 issuer links on the three rate pages.

No deal link was confirmed DEAD (all 8 came back inconclusive, bot-walled with HTTP 403). No `deals.ts` edit was made. No deal's `expiresAt` is more than 90 days before today (cutoff 2026-08-21), so no housekeeping removal either. This is a report-only run.

Twenty-two DEAD links were found, all in `src/data/news.ts`. News is an append-only archive and was not edited; these are reported for a human to review. Fourteen are new findings from this run; eight were already flagged in prior reports and are still dead. This run checked every `sourceUrl` instead of a sample, which is why the new-finding count is higher than recent days: most of the new dead links share a pattern of pointing at the issuer's own evergreen or generic landing page (a "your account" page, a "promotions" hub, an "advisories" status page) for what was actually a short, dated promotion. The page itself loads fine, so it never 404s, but it no longer shows the specific bonus, sale, or status the article cited, so a reader clicking "Read it at the source" today finds nothing confirming the story. Per the sentinel's standing rule (`docs/CRON_RUNBOOK.md` precedent from the Nov 14/17/18 reports), that counts as dead for sourcing purposes even though the HTTP status is 200.

## Everything else

- 8 deal links (no-`expiresAt` deals in `deals.ts`): all INCONCLUSIVE, HTTP 403 bot-wall (Newegg.ca, BestBuy.ca). No action taken.
- 52 of 100 news `sourceUrl` links: confirmed LIVE, substantiating the item they were cited for.
- 26 of 100 news `sourceUrl` links: INCONCLUSIVE (HTTP 403/5xx, fetch/parser errors, empty or truncated responses, un-hydrated template placeholders, a cookie-consent wall, or a redirect into a login flow). No action taken on news (append-only archive) either way.
- 17 of 17 rate-page issuer links (GIC, savings, chequing pages): 12 LIVE, 5 INCONCLUSIVE (EQ Bank `/rates` and `/` both truncated/unconfirmed, Simplii and Tangerine GIC-rate pages showing unrendered rate-template placeholders, BMO new-account-offers page returning HTTP 503). No DEAD rate-page links. No action taken.

Inconclusive results are expected: most merchant, bank, and airline sites bot-wall cloud fetches, and an HTTP 403/429/5xx, a login/consent wall, or an un-hydrated page is treated as unknown, not dead, per the sentinel's rules.

A pattern worth a human's attention: several WestJet and Air Canada `sourceUrl`s point at live, currently-updating pages (`get-travel-ready/advisories`, `guest-updates`, `your-aeroplan.html`, `aeroplan/promotions.html`, `special-offers.html`) that are reused as the source for more than one dated news item. Because these pages only ever show today's status or today's promotion, every older item sourced to one of them is likely to go dead on its own once the specific promotion or advisory it cited rolls off, through no fault of the page itself. A human reviewer may want to re-source those older items to an archived press release instead.
