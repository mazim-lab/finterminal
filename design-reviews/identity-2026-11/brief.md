# FinTerminal identity 2026-11: brief

## Positioning
FinTerminal is the family kitchen table where a household's money gets sorted out over tea.
One experienced churner dad, helping a friend: which card to get, how to turn points into a
trip, and how to set up the accounts. It should feel familiar, safe and generous. It is not a
trading terminal, not a fintech dashboard and not a research tracker. The name stays; the
feeling around it changes.

## What we move away from (FAEConf traits)
Inter + IBM Plex Mono, monospace uppercase labels, the three-colour header bar, the ink-square
logo with coloured bars or dots, dense zebra tables, ticker and "wire" styling, small radii and
hairline boxes everywhere. Only the colour VALUES stay.

## Mood
Homely: lived-in, unhurried, a little handmade. Abundance: a full pantry, a table with more than
enough, a garden bed that gives back. Growth reads as nourishment, not speculation. Rewards are
good things gathered over time, set out on shelves.

## Colour (kept values, new balance)
- Same tokens, light and dark. No new hues.
- Indigo leads (logo, primary buttons, links on hover, Cards). Cyan is Travel, fuchsia is Money.
  Cyan and fuchsia never sit as text on white; their text-safe variants do.
- Colour moves from thin rules and dots into large soft tinted "rooms": the pale indigo,
  cyan and fuchsia backgrounds become whole surfaces (hero, doors, shelves, notes).
- Status keeps its meaning: green fresh or gain, amber aging, red loss or expired.
- Warmth comes from type, shape, space, illustration and words, because the palette is cool.

## Type
- Headings: Fraunces (SIL OFL 1.1), a soft old-style serif with a SOFT axis; set at SOFT 100,
  optical size on, weight 600. Friendly, bookish, a cookbook more than a terminal.
- Text and UI: Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL 1.1), a humanist sans that stays very readable at small
  sizes and has true tabular figures.
- Numbers (money, rates, cpp, dates in tables) use Source Sans 3 with tabular figures, never a
  monospace face. Labels are sentence case, small and semibold, never tracked-out capitals.
- Both via next/font (self-hosted, size-adjusted fallbacks, no layout shift).
- Bigger scale: body 18px prose, generous line height, display up to 56px.

## Shape and space
- Rounder: 12px controls, 20px cards, 28px rooms, pill buttons and chips.
- Softer edges: fewer hairline boxes, more soft shadows and tinted fills.
- Generous space: sections are "rooms" separated by air, not rules.
- Cards feel like recipe cards or labelled jars: a soft tinted label strip, a title in the serif,
  a short note, then the facts.
- Tables stay scannable: sentence-case headers, roomy rows, quiet dividers, no bands.

## Illustration language
Small original line drawings: 2.5 unit round-capped strokes in the text colour, with soft
tinted fills from the three pale backgrounds, on a 120 unit grid, rounded joins, no outlines on
fills, no gradients, no faces. Subjects: pantry shelf of jars, a basket of good things, a
kettle and cup, a windowsill with a plant, a garden bed, a set table, an open door.
Used in hub headers, empty states, the 404 and the share image. Decorative only (aria-hidden).

## Logo concepts
1. Homestead sprout (shipped). A soft-cornered house with a two-leaf sprout growing from its
   floor, cut out of the solid shape. Home and growth in one silhouette; reads at 16px; works
   in one colour because the sprout is negative space.
2. Harvest bowl. A round bowl heaped full, with a sprig rising from the heap. Pure abundance,
   but it reads less clearly as "home" and gets muddy at 16px.
3. Pantry jar. A preserving jar two thirds full with a small maple leaf on the label. Saving as
   putting up food for winter, with a restrained Canadian note; the lid and leaf are fussy at
   favicon size.

Wordmark: "FinTerminal" stays, set in Fraunces 600 with the soft axis on, one colour, no grey
second half.

## Home page
A front door, not a dashboard: a warm welcome and who this is for; three doors (pick a card,
plan a trip on points, sort out your money); what is fresh from the kitchen (news, sweet spots,
deals, all with their real dates); where to start; the numbers I trust, with their stamps.
