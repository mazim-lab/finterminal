# Logo concepts (identity 2026-11)

Shipped: **1-homestead-sprout.svg**. The other two are kept here so the owner can ask for a swap.
`concept-sheet.png` shows all three at 16, 32 and 128 px.

1. **Homestead sprout (shipped).** A soft-cornered house with a two-leaf sprout growing from its
   floor, cut out of the solid shape. Home and growth in one silhouette. The sprout is negative
   space, so the mark works in one colour and still reads at 16 px.
2. **Harvest bowl.** A round bowl heaped full, with a sprig rising from the heap. The most direct
   picture of abundance, but it reads less as "home" and starts to look like a piece of fruit at
   favicon size.
3. **Pantry jar.** A preserving jar two thirds full, with a small maple leaf on the label. Saving
   as putting food up for winter, plus a restrained Canadian note. The lid and leaf get fussy at
   16 px.

To swap: replace `MARK_PATH` in `src/components/Logo.tsx`, `src/app/icon.svg`, the `MARK` path in
`src/app/api/og/route.tsx`, and regenerate the PNG icons in `public/icons/` and `src/app/favicon.ico`.
